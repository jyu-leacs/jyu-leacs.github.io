## Access to the content

This site uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, and responsive layouts. Animation respects your device’s reduced-motion preference. The seminar programme and articles remain available without JavaScript.

If a page or seminar resource is difficult to access, use the [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | url }}) to request another format when the organizers’ contact address is available.

## Your information

LEACS does not add its own analytics or first-party cookies. The contact page and workshop registration pages include Microsoft Forms embeds. Loading a form connects your browser to Microsoft, which may use cookies or other storage for its service. When you submit a form, Microsoft processes your answers and makes them available to the form’s owners under their organization’s Microsoft 365 configuration. Responses are not stored in this website’s GitHub repository. See [Microsoft’s privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

You can also contact us by email. The email link opens your email application; you write and send your message there. If the embedded form is difficult to use, the contact page includes a direct link to open it on Microsoft Forms and an email alternative.

The site loads its typography from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request the font files. System fonts are used if the files are unavailable. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting infrastructure may process request information under GitHub’s privacy policy.

Links to recordings, registration platforms, and other external resources may take you to services with their own privacy policies.
