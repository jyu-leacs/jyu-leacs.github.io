## A conversation across disciplines

LEACS is a seminar series about minds and the technologies we live with. We bring together perspectives from cognitive science, human–computer interaction, design, and related fields to explore questions that benefit from more than one point of view.

Our ambition is simple: make room for thoughtful research, open questions, and useful conversation.

## What we explore

- **Cognition & behaviour** — perception, attention, learning, decision making, and the ways people make sense of the world.
- **Human–computer interaction** — how people experience, use, and shape interactive systems.
- **Human-centred technology** — the relationship between people, intelligent systems, and design.
- **Ideas in between** — methods, theories, and emerging questions that connect these fields.

## Join the conversation

Students, researchers, designers, and anyone curious about these topics are welcome to explore the programme. Each talk page includes its participation details, language, venue, and timing when confirmed.

## Our research group

The Cognitive Science research group at the University of Jyväskylä studies how people think, feel, and interact with technology. Its work connects research on cognition and experience with computational models and methods for designing intelligent human–technology interaction.

The group examines cognitive and affective processes, the foundations of methods used to study users, and models that help explain and predict human behaviour with technology.
