## Session Info

PsychoPy is free, open-source software for creating experiments in Psychology and Neuroscience. It was created, and is still maintained, by scientists who use the tools themselves and is a community-led project with over 150 contributors. PsychoPy is widely used and has an increasing user base, currently sitting at more than 40,000 users a month. To ensure the project is sustainable, the team behind PsychoPy created a small company, Open Science Tools, which now has a revenue stream from additional platforms — Pavlovia.org for running studies online — and consultancy services.

Dr. Rebecca Hirst is a researcher based in Psychology and Chief Science Officer at Open Science Tools. In this workshop, she will walk through what is possible with PsychoPy and Pavlovia. She will aim to cover a broad range of possibilities, from making a simple experiment, to adding code components for flexibility, to integrating hardware such as eye tracking. She will also touch on Pavlovia.org as a hosting service for online studies, as well as a place to build surveys and questionnaires for online implementation.

We hope this walkthrough gives you a broad overview of what is possible with PsychoPy and Pavlovia, and that you leave wanting to give the tools a try!
