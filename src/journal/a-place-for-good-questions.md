## Where perspectives meet

Some questions become more interesting when we ask them together. How do people think? How do they interact with technology? How might a different perspective change the way we approach our research?

LEACS makes space for those conversations. Our focus brings cognitive science and human–computer interaction together, while leaving the door open to related disciplines.

## A journal alongside the talks

This journal can hold seminar summaries, reading notes, announcements, and open questions. Each post is a Markdown file, so sharing the next idea is as simple as writing a short piece of text.
