## Curiosity is a useful starting point

A seminar does not need to end with a definitive answer. Sometimes it gives us a better question, a useful connection, or a way to look at a familiar problem differently.

## Before the next talk

Consider a question from your own work. What assumption does it rely on? What might a researcher from another field notice? Bring that question into the discussion.

After the seminar, write down one idea you would like to explore further. The journal is a place to keep that conversation going.
